========================================================================
SUMMARY: WHAT HELPED AND WHAT DID NOT (median over 62 districts)
========================================================================
1. Log scale (rainfall, long-range, 2015-2019): error 63.3% vs 55.2% for plain SARIMA (+15%).
2. 2004 step (rainfall, long-range), change in error vs plain SARIMA: 2005-2009 -16.7%, 2010-2014 -0.9%, 2015-2019 -0.2%.
   A big gain in the earliest window and little later would mean the step matters mostly
   soon after a break; once the model has seen several post-break years it adds little.
3. Outside predictors, temperature (gradient boosting, change in error): 2005-2009 -3%, 2010-2014 -8%, 2015-2019 +5%.
3. Outside predictors, rainfall (gradient boosting, change in error): 2005-2009 -7%, 2010-2014 +10%, 2015-2019 -1%.
4. Average of SARIMA and gradient boosting vs SARIMA alone, temperature: 2005-2009 -4.0%, 2010-2014 -1.9%, 2015-2019 -1.6%.
4. Average of SARIMA and gradient boosting vs SARIMA alone, rainfall: 2005-2009 -4.4%, 2010-2014 +3.0%, 2015-2019 -3.8%.
   The average was the best model in 5 of 6 window-and-variable cases.
Remember: these are medians over 62 districts; individual districts vary a lot, and
even small improvements should be described as small.
