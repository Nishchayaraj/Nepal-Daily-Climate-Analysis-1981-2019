========================================================================
SUMMARY
========================================================================
  1. Feature sensitivity: the zones are robust to dropping the monsoon share (1 district moves) but sensitive to the 2004 break (8 districts move when only 2004+ rainfall is used).
  2. Choice of k: silhouette favours k = 2 on the baseline features; on temperature + altitude only the best is k = 3.
  3. Stability at k = 3: mean agreement 0.75 (5th percentile 0.47); zone stabilities 0.84, 0.83, 0.93; 8 borderline districts.
  4. Official belts: all Terai districts land in the lowland zone; the disagreement is at the Hill/Mountain edge and among low hills. 18 of 62 districts disagree.
