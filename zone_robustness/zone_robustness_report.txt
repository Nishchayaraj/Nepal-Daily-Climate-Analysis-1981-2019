CHECK 5  A DIFFERENT YARDSTICK: error relative to the variability of the months
========================================================================
Error = RMSE divided by the standard deviation of the district's monthly values in
2015-2019. 0 = nearly perfect, 1 = no better than guessing the average month.
Why: the rainfall error used so far is a % of average rainfall, so it is inflated in dry
districts (Spearman correlation with mean monthly rainfall: -0.47, p < 0.001).
